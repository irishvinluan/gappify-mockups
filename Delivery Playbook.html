How the train cascade works

Epic Branches are continuous and never gated — any epic can be worked on anytime, in parallel, regardless of trains. Once development is done, it merges into Dev.

Merging is what waits. An epic only joins the shared branch once its train's turn comes up — a later train can share an environment with an earlier one, but it can only move into the next environment once the earlier train has already moved out of it.

Example: D and E's coding in their own Epic Branches can go alongside A, B, C — but D and E can't be merged into Dev until A, B, C have moved to QA.

Roles on the train

Conductor — PM
Decides which train each epic belongs to, in collaboration with the Boarding Officer. Every month, also decides that month's train date — anywhere between the 10th and 20th — and announces it during DSUs.
Boarding Officer — Tech Lead
Makes sure only epics assigned to that train actually board it — enforcing what the Conductor decided, and collaborating on which epics fit which train.

Passengers = the epics themselves.

Train cutoff & exceptions

PM sets a cutoff for each train — one environment + one date (e.g. Aug 10 train → cutoff: UAT by Aug 3). Every epic is expected to hit that environment by that date on its own.

Made the cutoff
Epic keeps moving on its own normal timeline — unaffected by any other epic on the same train.
Won't make the cutoff
Default: epic rides the next train — unless an exception is granted (see below).

Exception path:

  • Anyone can flag that an epic won't make the cutoff — ideally as early as possible, spotted at daily standups
  • PM + Tech Lead assess risk case-by-case — no fixed criteria, an open judgment call
  • Low risk → epic is granted its own individual exception date
  • Risky → default applies, epic rides the next train
  • No outer limit on how far an exception can stretch — it depends entirely on the risk assessment

The cutoff is per-epic, not per-train — other epics keep their own timeline regardless of what's negotiated for a late one.

Bug management within the pipeline

First question: can it wait for the next train?

PM decides what can and can't wait.

Can't wait — Hotfix Route
Blocks sign-off, customer-facing, or data risk. Fix in place, then backmerge down to every earlier environment still holding that train.
Can wait — Normal Route
Minor or low-risk. No backmerge — rides the next train naturally.
